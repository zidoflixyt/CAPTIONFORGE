How to capture your video's audio (zero external noise):
  1. Click Start Transcription — when the browser asks, choose "This Tab" and enable "Share tab audio". This pipes the video's own audio into your system output.
  2. The recognition engine listens through your default microphone, so keep speakers on at comfortable volume (or use a headset's loopback / "Stereo Mix" input for a noise-free feed).
  3. Press Play — live phrases appear with real timestamps as the video plays.
